FAQ

Residential Asphalt Paving Questions

What are the benefits of residential asphalt paving?

Residential asphalt paving creates a smooth, durable surface that's low maintenance and can improve your home's curb appeal. It holds up to everyday use and gives areas like driveways and walkways a clean, finished look.

Is asphalt a good option for driveways?

Yes. Asphalt provides a smooth driving surface designed for regular vehicle traffic, and it's relatively easy to maintain and repair, which makes it a practical choice for residential driveways.

How do I maintain an asphalt driveway?

Routine maintenance helps keep asphalt looking and performing well. In New Jersey's climate, sealing the surface and addressing wear early can help guard against cracking caused by seasonal temperature changes.

Can asphalt be used for walkways and patios?

Yes. In addition to driveways, asphalt can be used for walkways, patios, pool decks, porches, and sidewalks, giving different areas around your home a consistent, durable surface.

Can damaged asphalt be repaired?

Often, yes. Damaged sections can frequently be repaired without replacing the entire paved area. Reach out and we can discuss your specific situation and provide a free quote.
